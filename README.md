# CaseBridge

### AI-Assisted Guided Case Journey

> AI doesn’t decide what happened. It helps people explain what happened.

CaseBridge is an independent hackathon prototype designed to help people organize complex real-world situations into a structured, human-reviewable case journey.

## Problem

People often struggle to explain complicated disputes clearly and organize the documents and information connected to them.

Important information may be scattered across:

- Messages
- Receipts
- Agreements
- Bank records
- Photos
- Personal statements
- Notices and documents

CaseBridge helps organize this information step by step.

## How CaseBridge Works

1. Tell your story
2. Understand the situation
3. Identify missing information
4. Find documents that may help
5. Upload and organize documents
6. Clarify differences
7. Preserve important information
8. Prepare for human review
9. Track follow-up items

## Key Features

- Guided case journey
- Voice and text intake
- English and Telugu interface
- AI-assisted information extraction
- Source mapping
- Document guidance
- Document analysis
- Clarification workflow
- Contextual Ask CaseBridge assistant
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Court Notice Explainer
- Structured Intake Brief
- Print-friendly document output
- Demo mode

## Important Scope

CaseBridge is an independent hackathon prototype.

It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India.

CaseBridge is not a lawyer and does not provide legal advice, determine legal rights, verify document authenticity, predict case outcomes, or determine the truth of a dispute.

## Current Prototype

The current version uses simulated/mock AI and document-processing services for demonstration purposes.

Future versions could connect these interfaces to real AI, OCR, secure storage, authentication, and human-review infrastructure.

## Demo

The project includes a synthetic rental security-deposit dispute for demonstration.

### Example

₹30,000 security deposit

→ ₹25,000 returned

→ ₹5,000 reportedly retained for repairs

CaseBridge organizes the information and identifies items that may require clarification rather than deciding the dispute.
